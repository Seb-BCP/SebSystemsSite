Seb's Approach

Simplicity, Speed & Stability

I aim for a business process to be:

  1. Simple, intuitive to learn with minimal instructions required
  2. Fast and efficient at completing the businesses weekly tasks
  3. Reliable and stable.

I started building business systems while working for Blue Collar People. While the systems worked, they were slow, repetitive and manual. The admin staff were rushed and stressed about deadlines constantly. For some reason, the systems in place really bothered me, I knew there was a better way to do this. As I became familiar with all aspect of the business, I made many failed attempts of doing these tasks differently to be more efficient. Each failure gave me the feedback to improve and learn how build internal tools for businesses to complete their administration and operational tasks.

I learned that changing business systems poorly can be destabilising to the business operations, and avoiding this is the highest priority! To make sure the ship continues to keep sailing without stress. My first solution was the same approach most companies go for, a complete all-encompassing software package to solve our problems. But we could see this would lead to issues, they didn’t quite do all of what we were after, the process was different, we had an established way of doing things which would be completely abandoned. Essentially it was akin to tearing down the ship and using a new one. Instead I found the better solution to be was not in replacing the ship, but working on it, renovating it and adding engines to old sail boat. The existing system was a foundation to strengthen, not a problem to throw away. I asked myself a different question:

What is the smallest change that will result in the most time saved without destabilising the business?

That started with small projects in Blue Collar People and eventually modifying all aspects of the business operations. It’s the same system with serious upgrading, I never replaced what was being done, I provided the leverage to do it quicker and more reliably.

Evolving the system from a strong foundation rather than tearing it down and starting from scratch is the core of my approach.

With every improvement I’m looking for three practical outcomes:

  • SimplicityThe process should feel intuitive, without an instruction manual. Someone new should be able to learn it quickly and use it with confidence.
  • SpeedThe system should do the work quickly, remove repeated handling and give people time back for the work that needs their attention.
  • StabilityThe change should strengthen daily operations, not introduce chaos or put the critical work of the business at risk.

Before a full transfer, the upgraded process runs side by side with the existing system in day-to-day work. That proves it works operationally, exposes issues while the current process is still in place and gives the team confidence before it becomes the new standard.

I do not replace what works, I will help you evolve the system you have already built.
