Start with how work gets done

I look at the real steps, including manual work, workarounds, exceptions and frustrations, before deciding what needs to change.

Improve what already works

Existing software, spreadsheets and processes can often stay. The aim is to connect and simplify the useful parts.

Use the right level of technology

Technology is a tool, not the service. The answer may be an improved spreadsheet, connected software, a simple automation, an internal tool or custom software.

How we make the change work

01

Understand

Meet the business and learn how the work actually gets done, including manual steps, workarounds, exceptions and frustrations.

02

Decide what should change and what should stay. Aim for the simplest practical improvement.

03

Build the appropriate solution around the business. This may involve existing software, spreadsheets, automation or custom software.

05

Improve the system based on real-world use until it becomes a dependable part of the business.
