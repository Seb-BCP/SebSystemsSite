Problems I solve

Do these issues slow your business down?

The warning sign is usually not one dramatic failure. It is the same small friction appearing every day.

01

The same information is handled more than once

A paper form becomes a spreadsheet, then an entry in another program, followed by a manual cross-check against the original.

02

Important work is difficult to see or follow

Jobs, approvals and records sit across folders, inboxes and one person’s memory, so nobody has a clear current status.

03

Your software nearly fits — but not quite

Staff rely on side spreadsheets, notes and workarounds because generic software does not match the exact way your business operates.

What I build

Not another platform you have to work around.

The system is shaped around the process, staff and information your business already uses.

01

Digital forms and approvals

Collect information once, route it to the right person and keep a clear record of what has been completed.

02

Tailored internal tools

Focused admin tools, dashboards or lightweight CRMs built specifically for the job your current software does not handle well.

03

Connected systems

Pass information between forms, spreadsheets, databases and accounting tools instead of asking staff to copy it manually.

The process

A focused first version in weeks, not months.

01

Short phone call

You explain the process that is wasting time or causing problems. I tell you plainly whether I am likely to help.

02

Meet at your business

You show me the real forms, spreadsheets, software and exceptions. We agree on a focused plan.

03

Build and test

I build the working system in stages, then we meet again to test it and make the agreed adjustments.

04

Adopt and hand over

You pay the agreed build fee if you choose to put the system into use. I teach you or your staff how to operate it.
