Case example

Evolving Blue Collar People’s systems without replacing everything at once.

This example shows how separate registrations, client orders and timesheets were connected around two central systems: Work Force and the Blue Folder.

Part one

Registrations and client orders create the placement structure.

Worker registrations and client orders enter as separate branches. Work Force brings the worker profile and order together, then creates the correct timesheet placement in the Blue Folder.

Registrations, orders and placements

Online registrationsWorkers submit registration details.
Worker profilesProfiles are created from the registration data.
Client ordersOrders enter Work Force as a separate branch.
Work ForceWorker profiles and client orders are matched here.
Blue Folder timesheet placementsThe correct worker, client and job record are created.

Separate inputs converge in Work Force, which creates the placement structure used by the Blue Folder.

Part two

Approved timesheets enter the correct placement and become useful outputs.

Workers submit timesheets through an online form. A PDF report and batch emailer sends them to the relevant client for approval. The approved timesheet then enters the correct Blue Folder placement.

Timesheets, approvals and outputs

Online timesheet formWorkers submit their weekly hours.
PDF report + batch emailerReports are generated and sent for approval.
Client approvalThe relevant client signs off the timesheet.
Placement from Work ForceThe worker, client and job record already exists.
The Blue FolderApproved hours are matched to the correct placement and processed here.
Payroll filePrepared for payroll processing.
InvoicesClient charging outputs are prepared.
Sales reportsManagement reporting is generated.

The approved timesheet and the placement structure converge in the Blue Folder, which then branches into payroll, invoicing and reporting.

The Blue Folder is the central hub that matches approved timesheets to job placements and prepares payroll, invoicing and reporting outputs.

Start with one process that is wasting time.

The purpose is not to rebuild the entire business. It is to make one focused improvement that proves itself in daily operation.
