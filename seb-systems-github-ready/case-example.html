Part one

Registrations and client orders create the placement structure.

Worker registrations and client orders enter as separate branches. Work Force brings the worker profile and order together, then creates the correct timesheet placement in the Blue Folder.

Part two

Approved timesheets enter the correct placement and become useful outputs.

Workers submit timesheets through an online form. A PDF report and batch emailer sends them to the relevant client for approval. The approved timesheet then enters the correct Blue Folder placement.
